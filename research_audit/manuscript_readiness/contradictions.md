# Contradiction register

**Classes:**
- **Scientific:** the conflict changes what can be concluded.
- **Reporting:** the evidence is adequate but the wording or framing conflicts with it.
- **Acknowledged limitation:** the manuscript already concedes the point, but elsewhere states it more strongly.

Every fix below is a wording or reporting change. None needs a new experiment. Claim IDs refer to `claim_ledger.md`.

| ID | Conflict | Where (claim IDs) | Class | Smallest fix |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| K01 | **Title and framing vs provenance.** The title asks "What Does LEN-Small Measure?", and the abstract and introduction treat the archive as LEN-Small "plus three". But the 103-graph archive has not been established as the published 100-graph subset: membership and content differ (A02), and the stated selection rule contradicts the published tables (A01). | P01, P02, P13, P17, P19, P46 vs A01, A02 | Reporting (with scientific consequence: the population is undefined) | Retitle (blueprint §0). Refer throughout to "a 103-graph archive downloaded from the LEN project website". Move LEN-Small to the Data section as context. Replace "three additional events" with "differs in membership and content". |
| K02 | **"Beyond Scale" vs what the count models contain.** M0 contains log n and log m. M0q adds their squares and product, so log(m/n), mean degree and density are functions of the count features. "Scale" is never defined, and the paper and audit use "size" for n, for (n, m), for N_K and for E[N_K]. E[N_K] is a functional of the degree sequence: it depends on n, m and heterogeneity in incident-edge counts, not on "size" alone. | P01, P10, P41, P42 vs P22, A06 | Reporting | Define the terms once: node count n; edge count m; their ratio m/n (= mean degree), with density a function of n and m; degree-related properties (degree Gini, incident-edge-count distribution); arrangement (reciprocity, components, coreness). Use "count model" for M0/M0q and "beyond node and edge counts" instead of "beyond scale". Say E[N_K] is determined by the degree sequence. |
| K03 | **"Influences" vs predictive association.** No sentence of the manuscript uses "influence" causally, but causal-sounding verbs recur: "training … matters far more" (P11), "what the sampling mechanism makes predictive" (L55), "content corrects" size-atypical events (R04), "event influence" (R §A, a leverage diagnostic). All the evidence is predictive association under cross-validation. | P11, P39, R04; L55 | Reporting | Use "was associated with", "changed out-of-fold log loss by" and "per-event contribution to the mean difference". Keep "influence" only as the statistical term "influence on the estimate" if needed, and define it. |
| K04 | **BA called threshold-dependent vs BA cited as significant.** V and D state that BA gains are threshold-dependent and that BA is unstable at n ≈ 100 (±0.01–0.02 from tie-breaks, A04). Yet V cites "BA +0.070, Holm < 0.003" for B − A at K = 500 with ΔAUC +0.002, D cites "BA gains … remain Holm-significant", and the manuscript highlights the K = 1000 KK − FF ΔBA +0.056 whose interval excludes zero while AUC and log loss are equal (A05). | V07, D05; P09 vs A04, A05 | Reporting | Treat BA as a secondary decision metric everywhere. Report it with AUC and log loss and do not cite BA significance as added information when AUC and log loss do not agree. For P09, state "a decision-threshold difference; AUC 0.953 vs 0.951". |
| K05 | **Confirmatory-sounding statistics vs post-hoc selection.** `token_std` was singled out after the Study 1 results were examined on these events (R C.5). Holm adjustment across K (V, D) and the 7-contrast Holm family (R) are reported in ways that read as confirmation: "supported with multiplicity control" (D07), E3 "STRONGLY SUPPORTED", "the strongest result remains Holm-significant". Holm controls error within a family; it does not correct for choosing the feature from the same data. The manuscript itself calls E2 and E3 exploratory and uncorrected. | D07, V04, V05, R07 vs R C.5; P28 | Scientific | Label every `token_std` result and E3 as exploratory and post hoc, in text, tables and captions. Present Holm values as within-family descriptive adjustments only. Never use "confirm" or "establish" for them. State the selection history in Methods. |
| K06 | **Stability presented as a finding vs an expected sampling property.** "The measurement survives sampling" (ρ ≥ 0.995, V02/V10/D §6) is what a per-edge average-type statistic estimated from hundreds of edges should show. At K = 1000 a view also contains most of the edges of a typical campaign graph (campaign median 1,078 edges, Table I). Likewise "direction stable in 19/20 partitions" (R02) is insensitivity to re-partitioning the same events, not replication. | V02, V10, D07, R02, P05 | Reporting | Present view-to-full agreement as a measurement check in Methods or the supplement, with the expected-property sentence. Present partition counts as "insensitivity to partitioning (same events)". |
| K07 | **"Neither … content alone (C3) accounts for this gain" and "complementary information"** vs the single-feature analysis: counts plus `token_std` show no detectable log-loss difference from M4 (R10, post hoc), and the C3 null reflects dilution by two redundant content features. | P27, P28 vs R10, R11, R C.5 | Scientific (the interpretation of the Study 1 gain changes) | Report the C3 result as "three-feature content set, no detectable change". Report the single-feature result as post hoc. Delete "complementary information … rather than a modelled interaction". |
| K08 | **"Topology contributes little or nothing detectable"** (R Part I) vs E2: structure given counts plus content −0.059 [−0.110, −0.011] over 20 partitions, and −0.091 [−0.182, −0.018] on the primary partition. A null performance contrast also cannot show that topology carries no information. | R17 vs R08, P28 | Scientific | Use "structural features added to counts alone (M2 − M0q) showed no detectable log-loss change; given content, they changed log loss by −0.059 [−0.110, −0.011] (exploratory)". Never write "topology contains no information". |
| K09 | **"Training regime mattered more than any feature choice"** vs A05: the struct6 retraining effect mixes regime with features that track N_K in fixed-K views (AUC 0.767 full vs 0.947 view-trained), and for N_K the frozen-transfer loss is a threshold collapse (BA 0.500, AUC 0.942). | P11, P39 vs A05, A13 | Scientific | Use the P11 proposed wording, and report AUC next to every transfer and retraining BA contrast. |
| K10 | **Study 2 content reported in BA only.** The manuscript reports "intervals that include zero at every budget" for BA. The log-loss intervals of the same contrast exclude zero at K = 500 (−0.127 [−0.226, −0.036]) and K = 1000 (−0.173 [−0.247, −0.105]). | P38 | Reporting (selective metric) | Report BA and log loss, uncorrected, for the three-feature content contrast. Keep it numerically separate from the `token_std` contrasts (X8). |
| K11 | **"Survives excluding the two events without text"** (R Part I) vs R C.3: C1 without them is −0.083 [−0.171, +0.008]. | R18 | Reporting | Report the interval. Only E3 (−0.087 [−0.158, −0.015]) excludes zero without them. |
| K12 | **"Counts plus that single feature … statistically indistinguishable from M4"** reads non-significance as equivalence. | R10 | Reporting | Use "no detectable difference (−0.014 [−0.055, +0.024]); equivalence not tested". |
| K13 | **Primary partition "shows the smallest" C1 BA gain** vs a tie with seed 5. | P29, P44 | Reporting | Use "tied for the smallest". |
| K14 | **Two uncertainty statements for C1 with different families** (Holm 0.009 over three primary tests on partition 42, vs Holm 0.12 over seven repeat-averaged contrasts). | K15 | **"The arrangement features showed no detectable improvement over the null"** is stated without its blind spot: the null preserves every incident-edge count, hence E[N_K] and any other degree-sequence functional, exactly. | P08 vs A06 | Reporting | Add one sentence in Methods: the null cannot detect signal carried by the degree sequence. |
| K16 | **`token_std` interpreted as templating or coordination** ("consistent with templated or copy-pasted campaign messages"; "substantive finding for coordination research"). | R15 | Scientific (the construct claim is unsupported) | Describe it only as "the standard deviation of whitespace token counts of retained edge texts".
